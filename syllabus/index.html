Course Description

Spot the analytics in your everyday life

Tap each card. Every one of these is a data-driven decision someone made about you — which is exactly what this course is about.

🎬 Netflix picks your next show
A recommender model predicts what you'll watch from millions of viewing patterns. (We cover this: Association Rules & Collaborative Filtering.)
🗺️ Maps knows your ETA
Live data + prediction models estimate arrival time and re-route you in real time.
💳 A fraud alert pings your phone
A classifier flags a transaction as "likely fraud" — someone tuned the cost of a false alarm vs. a missed fraud. (We cover this: Evaluating Performance.)
🎧 Spotify Wrapped
Clustering + summary analytics turn a year of listening into a shareable story. (We cover this: Cluster Analysis + Visualization.)
🛒 "Customers also bought…"
Market-basket analysis finds items that sell together and nudges the next purchase.
🏠 A home's "estimated value"
A regression model predicts price from features like size, location, and rooms. (This is literally one of our homework datasets!)

💬 Turn & talk: which one surprised you? Where else have you been on the receiving end of an analytics decision?

Purpose & Learning Outcomes

What this class is really about

By the end of the semester, you'll look at any business problem and answer three questions:

1

What decision am I informing?

Start with the choice a manager has to make — not the data.

2

Which method fits?

Regression? Classification? Clustering? Pick the right tool for the question.

3

What should we do about the result?

Turn the output into a recommendation someone can act on.

The rhythm of the course: you'll run the analysis in R (homework), then write it up for a decision-maker (case analysis). Run the model first — then tell the decision-maker what to do about it.
